Sacred Geography

The praise poem serves as a map of our heritage. These are the sacred landmarks and mountains that define the Muponda legacy.

Mt. Ruunji

"Gomo ringobuda mhute"

The sacred Mountain of Mist. The resting place of our Kings (Madzimambo). Only the strong can scale it.

Nyazongo

"Wari Nyazongo"

The spiritual home where the visionary ancestor Samaringa rests.

Chikwira chaSaka

"The Hill Palace"

A place of immense respect. Tradition dictates it must be climbed while kneeling.

Watsomba

"PaMutsamvu weWatsomba"

The site of the great Fig Tree (Mutsamvu) where remembrance ceremonies are held.

Our Historical Journey

Origins in Tanganyika

Our oral tradition confirms that the "Dzinde" (the root/origin) is in Tanganyika (modern-day Tanzania/Ruvuma region).

The Great Migration

The ancestors traveled south, crossing the Kasambavezi (Zambezi), Gorongoza, and Rwenya rivers.

Settlement in Manyika

The dynasty established itself in Manyika, with sacred sites at Ruunji and Nyazongo.
